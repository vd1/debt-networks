Risk approach
YTD

YTD maps what yield depends on, recursively.

The output is not a label like "PYUSD" or "stETH carry". It is a live dependency tree across vaults, collateral, lending markets, wrappers, oracles, and off-chain boundaries.

Once the tree exists, risk measures and shock propagation models can sit on top of it.

Where YTD stops
syrupUSDC

syrupUSDC is the clean Bluprynt bridge example.

It appears inside the PYUSD and senPYUSD YTD, but the graph stops once it enters the off-chain issuer and collateral layer.

This is the exact join point: YTD finds the dependency, Bluprynt can attach issuer, reserve, certificate, collateral, and disclosure state.
