\subsection{Verifications (corrections to first-pass claims)}\label{verifications-corrections-to-first-pass-claims}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Aperture Finance loss:} \textbf{\$3.67M} for Aperture alone. The widely-cited \textbf{\$17M} figure is the \emph{combined} loss across \textbf{SwapNet (\$13.43M) + Aperture (\$3.67M)} --- same root cause (arbitrary-call + unsanitized \texttt{transferFrom} on pre-existing approvals), same day (2026-01-25, \textasciitilde5:10pm UTC, on Ethereum/Arbitrum/Base/BSC). Treat as two line items or as a joint ``SwapNet+Aperture \$17M'' incident.
\item
  \textbf{CrossCurve date:} \textbf{2026-01-31 → 2026-02-01 UTC.} Vector: spoofed Axelar messages via unvalidated \texttt{expressExecute} in ReceiverAxelar.
\item
  \textbf{IoTeX (ioTube) date:} \textbf{2026-02-21.} Compromised Ethereum-side Validator key → TokenSafe drained (\$4.3M USDC/USDT/IOTX/WBTC/BUSD) + 111M CIOTX + 9.3M CCS minted; PeckShield total \textasciitilde\$8M.
\item
  \textbf{``Morpho TVL \$10B→\$7B tied to Resolv'':} \textbf{claim is mis-attributed.} The \$10B→\$6B figure (Chorus One) describes \textbf{risk-curator-managed TVL across DeFi}, and the contraction is dominantly \textbf{Nov 2025 Stream-driven}, not Resolv. Morpho itself had limited \emph{direct} xUSD exposure (\textasciitilde\$68M Plume private + \$628K Arbitrum public). Resolv hit \textasciitilde15 Morpho vaults; Gauntlet's USDC Core was the most concentrated (\$4.95M in wstUSR/USDC = 98\% of that market's lender liquidity). MORPHO token fell 4.6\% on Resolv, not 30\%.
\item
  \textbf{MakinaFi root cause:} \textbf{permissionless oracle manipulation}, not just ``MEV builder.'' \texttt{updateTotalAum()} was public, pulled live Curve spot without TWAP/circuit breaker, and could be moved within the same tx. Flow: flash-loan \$280M USDC (Morpho + Aave) → dump into Curve → call \texttt{updateTotalAum()} → withdraw DUSD at inflated NAV → repay. Cantina audit competition had this exact attack class explicitly out-of-scope.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Analytical notes}\label{analytical-notes}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{No standalone Morpho Blue contract exploit in the window.} Morpho's exposure was always second-order --- curator allocations (Stream/xUSD, Elixir/deUSD, Resolv/USR, Kelp/rsETH) and market-listing/oracle governance. \emph{That is itself the finding.} Lines up directly with the four ``illusions'' in \texttt{QFTaskList.tex} (collateral independence / TVL / diversification / leverage).
\item
  \textbf{Best Morpho-risk case studies in the set:} Stream Finance (Nov 2025) --- every pathology in one episode. Moonwell (Feb 2026) --- cleanest oracle-wiring case. KelpDAO (Apr 2026) --- cleanest looping-contagion case. Aevo Dec 2025 and YieldBlox Feb 2026 are clean oracle-config cases at smaller scale.
\item
  \textbf{``Dark loops'' thesis:} KelpDAO Apr 2026 + Stream Nov 2025 both show the pattern --- single collateral/vault failure amplified by cross-lender looping produces a far larger TVL deletion than the face-value exploit.
\end{itemize}
