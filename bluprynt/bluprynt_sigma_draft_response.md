# SIGMA LABS × BLUPRYNT

Response to Proposed Engagement: Sigma Labs Terms

Indicative term sheet, subject to MNDA and to the Bluprynt PoC revenue projections requested in §12. Bracketed figures are placeholders to be confirmed against those projections.

We're aligned on the vision: a model-backed, regulator-grade collateral primitive, with a clean model-versus-code boundary. The structure below keeps that boundary intact: Bluprynt builds, owns and operates the stack; Sigma supplies and continuously maintains the loss models that make the disclosure bankable. We're glad to confirm the MNDA, the shared working doc, and the Plume Nest reference pilot as immediate next steps.
The points below respond to your seven-step proposal in order.

1. Engagement shape: research partnership, not acquisition
A research partnership, structured so Sigma's scarce expertise is paid as research and the model layer remains a continuous obligation by design, because a loss model is recalibrated by its quant authors as markets, protocols and depeg events evolve, not frozen and maintained as code. This is the premise the rest of the terms rest on.

2. Onboarding & knowledge-transfer fee (your Step 01)
A one-time fee covering the joint technical review, the integration of Sigma's current on-chain engine into the Bluprynt stack, and the methodology transfer, i.e. the shared deep-dive, adapter handoff, and bringing the Bluprynt team up the curve. [Indicative: €75k.] This compensates the initial collaboration and recognises that Sigma arrives with a system already in production and a live institutional reference (see §11).

3. Research fee + annual minimum (your Step 02a): accepted
A fixed annual research fee with a stated minimum, covering the Schedule A deliverables and their ongoing maintenance: model improvement, recalibration to new protocols and depeg events, and calibration of the monitoring rules and materiality thresholds. The minimum is due regardless of resale volume, so the scarce expertise is paid as research, not only as a sales channel. [Indicative: €300k / year.]

4. Model royalty, platform-wide (your Step 02b): accepted in principle, to be sized
A model royalty on the premium / insurance-tier PoC revenue that Sigma's loss models enable (platform-wide, including Bluprynt's own accounts) since, as your own materials state, the insurance-bankable tier does not exist until someone produces the loss models that price it. We accept this term but ask that "modest" be replaced by a defined percentage. [Indicative: 10% of premium / insurance-tier PoC revenue, to be confirmed against the projections in §12.] This, alongside §3, is Sigma's primary economics. Because the royalty is platform-wide, Bluprynt provides periodic revenue reporting and reasonable audit rights to verify the premium / insurance-tier base on which it is calculated.

5. Model IP, retained by Sigma (your Step 06, first half): affirmed
Sigma retains full ownership of its loss-propagation methodology and models, and may apply them outside collateral disclosure. Bluprynt receives a licence to implement them within PoC and owns the resulting integration code. Ownership of the models themselves does not transfer.

6. Technology: Bluprynt keeps the code; Sigma keeps a usage right
The engine, graph, adapters and monitoring stack stay with Bluprynt, with no permanent dependency on Sigma in the code or monitoring path, and we agree this is the right posture for regulator credibility. In exchange, Sigma retains a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use the on-chain engine (the yield-tree / YTD) for its own non-competing activities: specifically Ellen Capital's fund and curator activity and Sigma's own (free) research partnerships. This usage right is bounded only by the field defined in §7.

7. Non-compete & non-circumvention (your Step 06): bounded
Mutual non-circumvention: accepted.
Non-compete: accepted only if bounded on all four axes, and binding both sides symmetrically:
Commercial premise: this is a two-technology, two-market collaboration. Bluprynt brings PoC, the regulator / issuer interface, and its collateral-disclosure distribution. Sigma brings YTD / DIG, loss models, and its fund, curator, research and data distribution. Each party remains free to sell its own technology standalone in its own market. Each party may also sell the other party's technology as part of a combined offer, with revenue share only on that combined sale. The non-compete should therefore prevent selling against each other in the other party's home market, not block either party's standalone business.
Purpose / finality: issuer- and regulator-facing collateral disclosure for tokenized RWAs, and nothing else. It must not reach Sigma's or Ellen Capital's fund, curator, research or data activities.

Sector: tokenized-RWA collateral disclosure only.
Time: limited to the agreement term plus a 12-month tail; not perpetual.
Geography: [as applicable]; we note that for a digital product the purpose and sector bounds are the operative ones.

8. Channel economics (your Step 04): accepted, secondary
We accept the non-exclusive resale licence and a 30% revenue share to Sigma, net of pass-through data and monitoring COGS, on accounts Sigma sources, with the trailing share on those accounts surviving wind-down. Symmetrically, if Bluprynt sources an account for a combined Sigma-plus-Bluprynt product in Sigma's home market, Bluprynt should receive the agreed channel share. No revenue share applies when either party sells its own technology standalone. Noted as secondary to §3–§4.

9. Acceptance & validation (your Steps 01 / 03, Schedule A): accepted
Deliverables gated on documented assumptions, sensitivity analysis, backtest against named historical depeg events, and stated false-positive / false-negative rates, each adopted into production only on a met accuracy / calibration threshold. We're aligned that pay should reward model quality, not volume.

10. Reference pilot: Plume Nest (your Step 05): yes
A live nested-vault asset with off-chain components as the first integrated deployment: Bluprynt's full asset-plus-yield graph and monitoring, with Sigma's loss models validated end to end on a real structure.

11. Institutional reference
The "institutional allocator in production" referenced in your materials is Ellen Capital, of which I am founder. We're glad for it to serve as a reference and will align on how it is described in any joint or marketing materials.

12. To calibrate: requests before we fix figures
Bluprynt's PoC premium / insurance-tier revenue projections, so the annual minimum (§3) and the royalty (§4) are sized against real economics rather than in the abstract.
Mutual confirmation of code-IP ownership at handoff (clean title to the on-chain engine on both sides before integration).
Term, renewal and survival language for the royalty and the §6 licence-back.
Next steps: countersign the MNDA, open the shared working doc, and tailor the Plume Nest demo, then formalise the above into an agreement.
